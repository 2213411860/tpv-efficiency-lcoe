Lin model point

TPV prediction based on the Si cell parameters of Lin et al.; not a TPV measurement in the original paper. Selected at the maximum baseline LCOE among 51 previously calculated temperatures (1600–2100 K, 10 K intervals), using LCOH=1 ¢/kWhth, CPA=0.2 $/cm², CF=1 and CRF=0.074.

The selected temperature is fixed. Selective emitter: εin=0.7, εout=0.1; view factor: 0.5; cell temperature: 300 K; variable-Rs three-diode model.

Calculation notes

LCOE = 1000 × 0.074 × CPA / (8760 × CF × Pout) + (LCOH / 100) / (η / 100).

CPA values are comparison assumptions. Only cell capital and heat costs are included. Literature coordinates are retained from the original dataset; publication links identify the original studies.

Bubble area: A=12+1788u³ pt², where u is inverse-normalized log₁₀(LCOE). The fixed scale spans the original dataset; out-of-range values are clipped in size only.
