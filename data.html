Si HJT TPV model prediction

The TPV performance is modeled using published Si HJT solar-cell parameters; no cell fabrication or TPV measurement is reported for this model point. Selected at the maximum baseline LCOE among 51 previously calculated temperatures (1600–2100 K, 10 K intervals), using LCOH=1 ¢/kWhth, CPA=0.2 $/cm², CF=1 and CRF=0.074.

The selected temperature is fixed. Selective emitter: εin=0.7, εout=0.1; view factor: 0.5; cell temperature: 300 K; variable-Rs three-diode model.

Calculation notes

CPA values are comparison assumptions. Only cell capital and heat costs are included. Literature coordinates are retained from the original dataset; publication links identify the original studies.

Bubble size uses a fixed absolute-LCOE scale. The same LCOE gives the same circle size across scenarios; lower LCOE produces a larger circle.
